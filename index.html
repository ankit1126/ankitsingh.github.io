About

I study how firms, markets, and policies shape who gets access to services, and what that means for customers, employees, and firm value.

My substantive work spans healthcare marketing and access to services, consumer and employee well-being, AI and technology in marketing strategy, and sustainability strategy. Methodologically, I combine causal inference with archival and field data, machine learning, and natural language processing.

Before the Ph.D., I earned an MBA from the Institute of Rural Management Anand, worked as a research assistant at IIM Udaipur, and began my career as a business analyst at Accenture.

Research

Job market paper

The Residual Segment: How Market Segmentation Shapes What Customers Receive in Health Care

Ankit Singh, Amalesh Sharma, and Sarang Sunder

Job market paperPaper available on request

Firms and regulators often pool customers who fit no core market into a residual segment. Does belonging to this segment change the service customers receive? We study a 2023 reform that dissolved a residual insurance pricing region and merged its mostly rural counties into nearby metropolitan markets, while care stayed in the same hospitals. Using a difference-in-differences design and 1.97 million hospital admissions, we find that integration raised service use by 9.5%, increased the chronic conditions providers recorded by 3.6%, and cut seven-day mortality by 17%. Effects were largest for patients with the most varied needs. Segment boundaries shape what providers learn about customers and what customers receive.
